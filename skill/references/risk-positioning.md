# Risk and positioning module

Default to fixed fractional risk: `shares = floor(portfolio_value * risk_budget_pct / abs(entry - stop))`, then cap notional by concentration. Prefer structure or ATR stops; if no stop is supplied, return a sizing warning rather than inventing one. The engine now also reports a conditional fractional-Kelly capital cap when the fixed-rule trade log contains enough out-of-sample trades; that cap is an additional constraint, not a replacement for the stop-based loss budget.

Kelly is conditional, not automatic. The engine estimates an unlevered log-growth fraction from deterministic trade returns and exposes an exploratory estimate plus confidence when the sample is short. The standard validated tier requires at least 60 total trades and at least 30 reserved out-of-sample trades; a short-horizon tactical tier may show a conditional estimate from a declared smaller OOS block, but it remains an auxiliary signal and is not automatically applied to a position. Every report must still display the available `p`, `b`, raw Kelly, full-Kelly, half-Kelly, quarter-Kelly, option/event haircut, known caps, and `diagnostic_fractional_kelly_cap`; only `applied_fractional_kelly` is gated by validation. The engine includes configured transaction costs and discloses uncertainty. Full, half, and quarter are scenario outputs; quarter remains the conservative default cap only after fixed-risk and concentration caps are calculated. Option IV can haircut these fractions for volatility/event risk, but cannot supply directional edge or real-world probabilities. Do not feed a short or regime-specific single-stock backtest directly into automatic sizing.

For scenarios, define bear/base/bull assumptions, price or cash-flow mechanism, catalyst, probability as an explicit assumption, and invalidation. The engine does not issue a trade recommendation.
