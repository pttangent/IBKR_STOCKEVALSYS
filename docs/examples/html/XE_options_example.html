UNAVAILABLE_ZERO_OR_UNTRUSTED_OI: the option chain contains no economically meaningful non-zero OI from which to build a gross-gamma concentration map. The renderer must not rank zero-value strikes and must not display a fake upper/lower Gamma Wall.

What is still allowed

ObservedExpiry directory, last-trade premiums, volume distribution, numerical/approximate IV and total-variance diagnostics.
Not observedReliable OI concentration, signed dealer exposure, dealer hedge flow, Gamma Flip or a confirmed squeeze.
Conditional scenario onlyIf dealer sign were independently inferred later, short-gamma and long-gamma hedge tendencies could be modeled as separate scenarios.
